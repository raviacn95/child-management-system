Baby & toddler essentials checklist

Buy for the stage your child is in, not the next three. This list covers the basics most families use daily.

Newborn (0–3 months)

  • Soft cotton onesies and jhablas (6–8)
  • Muslin swaddles or wraps (4–6)
  • Diapers or cloth nappies, plus a gentle rash cream
  • Mild baby wash and unscented wipes
  • Firm, flat sleep surface with a fitted sheet
  • Burp cloths and bibs
  • Car seat if you travel by car

6–12 months

  • High chair or floor seat for meals
  • Steel or silicone bowls and soft spoons
  • Sippy or open cup for water
  • Teething toys
  • Board books
  • Socket covers and cabinet locks

Toddlers (1–3 years)

  • Comfortable shoes for walking
  • Stacking cups, blocks and shape sorters
  • Crayons and large paper
  • Potty seat when they show readiness
  • Leak-proof water bottle

Preschool (3–6 years)

  • School bag and steel lunch box
  • Puzzles (24–60 pieces) and picture story books
  • Child-safe scissors, glue and craft paper
  • Raincoat and a spare set of clothes for school

What you can usually skip

  • Wipe warmers, baby shoe collections, and most gadget "bundles".
  • Clothes more than one size ahead — children grow unevenly.

Always follow your paediatrician's advice on sleep, feeding and safety products. This list is general guidance.
