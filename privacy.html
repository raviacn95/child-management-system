Willow privacy notice

Willow keeps childcare records on this device only. Sign-in, child notes, attendance, and shop carts are not uploaded to Willow servers. Passwords and delivery addresses are not written into browser storage. Pickup PINs stay on the handoff desk and are not shown on child profiles.

Optional usage analytics is off by default. If you enable it in Settings, Willow sends only page visits, timestamps, a random installation ID, and a temporary session ID to a configured first-party analytics server. It does not send names, email addresses, child records, PINs, medical notes, ad identifiers, or precise location. You can withdraw consent in Settings; the installation ID is then deleted locally.

The Android app does not allow cloud backup of Willow data and will not open websites outside the official Willow site and the storefronts you tap. Movie or YouTube links open those companies under their own terms. Willow does not use advertising trackers.

Do not enter real children’s personal data in this public demo. If you later connect a live backend, that host’s privacy policy applies.
