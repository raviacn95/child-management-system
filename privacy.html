Willow privacy notice

Willow is a demo childcare operations app. In this public build, sign-in is local to your device (browser or Android app). Child records, attendance, and shop carts stay in on-device storage and are not uploaded to Willow servers.

If you connect a live backend later, that host’s privacy policy applies. YouTube links open Google’s services under their terms. Do not enter real children’s personal data in the demo.
