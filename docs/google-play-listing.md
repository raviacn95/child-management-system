# Willow Google Play listing draft

## App name

Willow Childcare Operations

## Short description

Childcare operations, family updates, learning packs, meals, attendance, and billing.

## Full description

Willow helps childcare centres, teachers, and families manage the day in one calm workspace.

Centres can organize children, attendance, secure pickup, daily care diaries, health records, meals, learning observations, staff schedules, documents, billing, inventory, and reports. Directors get role-based access and multi-site visibility. Teachers can record observations and care updates. Parents can follow approved updates, explore age-based learning packs, plan meals, and stay connected to home learning.

Willow is designed for practical childcare work:

- Role-based director, teacher, and parent portals
- Secure attendance and pickup workflows with audit history
- Daily meals, naps, diapers, mood, activities, and learning notes
- Health, immunization, medication, incident, and document records
- Billing, receipts, supplies, occupancy, and collection reports
- Age-based learning packs and co-viewing safeguards
- Country packs for India, UAE, Singapore, UK, and US workflows
- Optional Willow Coach for non-sensitive activity ideas
- Local-first storage in the public demo

Willow does not host or pirate video. External movie, YouTube, and storefront links open under those services’ own terms. Do not enter real children’s information in the public demo.
