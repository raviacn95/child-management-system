# Willow worldwide growth plan

The app cannot guarantee one million downloads through code alone. This plan turns the product into a measurable, privacy-respecting launch program.

## Launch sequence

1. India pilot: 10-20 childcare centres, English/Hindi, verify attendance, pickup, diary, meals, and billing workflows.
2. UAE and Singapore: add local compliance review, currencies, school calendars, language support, and partner onboarding.
3. UK and US: validate safeguarding, record retention, nutrition, consent, and data-processing requirements with counsel.
4. Global parent release: publish age-based learning packs and family planner content as the acquisition loop.

## Acquisition loops

- Centre invites: every enrolled family receives a parent invite and guided setup.
- Free centre starter: one site, limited active children, learning and diary included.
- Educator content: weekly co-view activities and printable meal/learning resources.
- Referral credits: reward centres for verified active families, not raw installs.
- Partnerships: preschool associations, pediatric dietitians, teacher-training providers, and local education events.

## Measurement

Track only consented, aggregate events through a privacy-reviewed analytics provider: install, activation, first attendance record, first parent invite, week-one return, and centre retention. Do not send child names, IDs, allergies, health notes, pickup PINs, or diary text to analytics or Grok.

## Release gates

- Crash-free sessions and startup time targets are met on low-cost Android phones.
- Support response process and deletion/export process are documented.
- Store listing is translated and screenshots contain no personal data.
- Each country pack has a local privacy and safeguarding review.
- Pricing, subscription, taxes, refunds, and Play Billing decisions are approved before monetization.
